THE PROBLEM

Small businesses buy at a structural disadvantage.

Less purchasing power. Limited access to supplier credit. Procurement spread across a dozen vendors. Sourcing and quoting done by hand, then several deliveries and several invoices for what should have been one order.

Traditional distribution solves part of this, and pays for it with inventory, working capital, warehouses and people. Every unit of growth costs another unit of capital. Ventu changes the architecture instead of the arithmetic.

THE ARCHITECTURE

Six layers. We own the two that compound.

Everything physical belongs to somebody else. What Ventu owns is the technology and the intelligence that decides how the physical capacity gets used.

  1. 01

    Demand

    Small orders become aggregated purchasing power.

  2. 02

    Supply

    Inventories are integrated rather than owned.

  3. 03

    Credit

    Third-party fintech finances the buyer, off our balance sheet.

  4. 04

    Fulfilment

    Specialised third parties operate under Ventu processes and technology.

  5. 05

    Logistics

    Third-party carriers provide the physical capacity.

  6. 06

    Intelligence

    Ventu coordinates the system and decides what happens next.

THE TECHNOLOGY

Turning fragmented wholesale supply into AI-ready commerce.

Ventu ingests catalogues, normalises SKUs, enriches missing specifications, connects stock and price changes, observes market signals, learns from every transaction and executes procurement through AI agents.

The stack runs twelve modules across catalogue, supply, search, pricing, channels, orders, fulfilment, logistics, billing, financing and intelligence, orchestrated by a single agent that reads intent in natural language.
